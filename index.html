Save my seat

The question every customer asks.

For data architects, ETL architects and the leaders who own an Informatica estate.

Can we modernize the estate without losing the logic the business depends on?

Informatica migration is a complexity problem.

Logic is buried

Transformations live inside mappings, mapplets, lookups, routers and expression logic.

Dependencies are hidden

Sessions, parameters, overrides, connections and wrappers change runtime behavior.

Testing is expensive

Manual test scripts and incomplete baselines make output equivalence hard to prove.

Cutover risk is high

A small semantic difference can create downstream data drift and business distrust.

Not a manual rewrite. An agentic migration factory.
